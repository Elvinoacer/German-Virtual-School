## **Why Germany?**

Germany is Europe's largest economy and one of the world's leading destinations for international students and professionals.

Some of the advantages include:

• Public universities with little or no tuition fees  
 • High demand for skilled workers

Learning German significantly increases your chances of accessing these opportunities.

# **German Language Programs**

German Virtual School offers structured courses covering the **entire Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR)** — from beginner to mastery.

Our programs are designed to help students progress from **basic communication to full professional fluency in German**.

Students preparing for internationally recognized exams administered by the Goethe-Institut — including those conducted locally at Goethe-Institut Nairobi — can use our courses to build the skills required for success.

Our programs follow the CEFR language levels:

A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 → C1 → C2

---

# **A1 German Course (Beginner)**

This course introduces students to the fundamentals of the German language.

Students learn how to:

• introduce themselves  
 • ask and answer simple questions  
 • understand basic everyday expressions  
 • read and write simple sentences

At the end of A1, students can communicate in basic everyday situations.

This level is often required for **family reunification visas and initial language certification**.

---

# **A2 German Course (Elementary)**

This level expands vocabulary and strengthens communication skills.

Students learn how to:

• participate in simple conversations  
 • understand frequently used expressions  
 • describe daily routines and experiences  
 • write short messages and emails

A2 prepares students for **more independent communication in everyday life**.

---

# **B1 German Course (Intermediate)**

B1 marks the transition to independent language use.

Students learn how to:

• express opinions and experiences  
 • understand longer conversations  
 • communicate in professional and social situations  
 • write structured texts and emails

B1 certification is commonly required for:

• vocational training programs (Ausbildung)  
 • many work visa pathways  
 • long-term residence requirements

---

# **B2 German Course (Upper Intermediate)**

B2 focuses on advanced communication skills and professional language use.

Students develop the ability to:

• discuss complex topics  
 • understand detailed written and spoken German  
 • participate confidently in workplace conversations  
 • produce clear and well-structured writing

B2 is often required for:

• university admission  
 • professional employment  
 • advanced vocational training

---

# **C1 German Course (Advanced)**

C1 prepares students for academic and high-level professional environments.

Students learn to:

• understand complex texts and lectures  
 • communicate fluently and spontaneously  
 • present arguments clearly and persuasively  
 • write detailed essays and reports

C1 is commonly required for:

• university degree programs in Germany  
 • research positions  
 • advanced professional roles

---

# **C2 German Course (Mastery)**

C2 represents near-native mastery of the German language.

Students at this level can:

• understand virtually everything they read or hear  
 • summarize complex information clearly  
 • express ideas with precision and nuance  
 • communicate at a professional or academic level

C2 proficiency is suitable for:

• academic research  
 • teaching German  
 • high-level professional environments
